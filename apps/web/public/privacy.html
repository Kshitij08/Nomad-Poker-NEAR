Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Nomad Poker is a free-to-play online poker game at www.nomadpoker.fun. This page explains what we store about you, why, who else handles it, and how to have it deleted. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

What we collect

  • Your account. A player ID, the display name and avatar you choose, your Friend ID and level. If you play as a guest, that is all.
  • How you sign in. With Google: your Google account ID, email address and first name, which Google shares when you agree on its sign-in screen. With email: your email address. With a Solana wallet: your wallet's public address. We never see your Google password or your wallet's private key.
  • Game activity. Your coin balance and every change to it, the hands you play (cards, actions and results), tournament entries, friends and invites, rewards you claim, and shop purchases.
  • Agents, your twin and the coach. The settings of any Agent you run, strategies you publish or copy, and the patterns your twin learns from your own decisions at Coins tables. The twin learns only from what you could see at the table; it never sees other players' hidden cards.
  • Technical data. Your IP address and browser type reach our servers with each request. We use them to keep the service running and to limit abuse (for example, too many sign-in attempts), and they appear briefly in server logs.
  • On your device. The game saves settings, your sign-in token and some progress in your browser's local storage. You can clear it from your browser at any time.

What we don't collect

  • No advertising or third-party analytics trackers. Game statistics stay on your device unless they are part of the activity above.
  • Voice chat, when you switch it on, connects your microphone directly to the other players at your table. Your voice is not sent to or recorded by our servers.
  • Chat messages at a table are shown to that table and are not kept after the table closes.

Why we use it

  • To run your account, keep your coins and progress, and let you sign in on other devices.
  • To run fair games: dealing hands, settling pots, checking the books, and investigating disputes or cheating.
  • To provide features you turn on, such as Agents, your twin, the coach, friends and tournaments.
  • To send the one-time sign-in links you ask for. We don't send marketing email.

Who else handles it

  • Cloudflare runs our servers and database and stores hand histories, and sends sign-in emails.
  • Vercel serves the website.
  • Google handles Sign in with Google, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Solana and MagicBlock networks. SOL tables currently run on Solana's devnet test network. Transactions there are public by design, including your wallet address.

These providers process data for us under their own security and privacy terms. Some of them process data outside your country.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and game records while your account exists, because your coins and history depend on them. Server logs are kept for a short time. Sign-in links expire after 15 minutes. Backups are kept for up to 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • You can change your display name and avatar in the game at any time.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, or for your account and its data to be deleted, by emailing support@nomadpoker.fun from the address on your account (or with your Friend ID). We may keep records we are legally required to keep.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as correcting your data or objecting to how we use it. Contact us and we will help.

Children

Nomad Poker is for adults. You must be at least 18, or the age of majority where you live if that is higher. We don't knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes we will also tell you in the game.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@nomadpoker.fun.
