Rollup wallet (devnet)

Deposits now happen in the game: Private Tables › Deposit puts SOL straight into your game balance, and Withdraw all sends it back. This page is for money deposited here before that (it sits in your own rollup wallet, not your game balance): check it and withdraw it. Phantom must be on devnet (Settings › Developer settings › Testnet mode).

Cost (measured on devnet): in the game, opening your game balance is a one-time 0.0126 SOL of account rent, the first deposit about 0.002 SOL more, and later deposits and withdrawals about 0.0005 SOL each; setting up withdrawals is a one-time 0.0113 SOL (already done if you deposited on this page before). On this page, the first deposit from a wallet paid about 0.0064 SOL of one-time rent.
